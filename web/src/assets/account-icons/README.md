# Account brand icons

The PNG files in this directory are 64 x 64 transparent bank, payment app, and
broker icons. Their editable or original sources are stored in `source/`.
General-purpose account icons are stored in the sibling `system-icons/`
directory. The account list selects icons from the database `accounts.icon`
value and falls back to the account type.

Bank marks are derived from the favicons published by each bank's official
website. Bank names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
Broker marks are derived from the favicons published by each securities
company's official website. Company names and marks remain the property of
their respective owners.

Payment marks are sourced from the corresponding Iconify brand collections.
Brand names and marks remain the property of their respective owners.
